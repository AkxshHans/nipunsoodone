        Questions
        Frequently asked
        
      
        
        
        Do I need to visit an office?
        No. Everything happens online, from sharing documents to approving your return. You can reach us on WhatsApp, by phone or by email.
        
        
        I'm new to Canada. Can you help with my first return?
        Yes. We'll explain what to report for the year you arrived and which benefits and credits you can start receiving.
        
        
        How long does it take?
        Most returns are ready within 24–48 hours once your documents are complete. Corporate returns can take longer, and we'll give you a timeline upfront.
        
        
        How much does it cost?
        It depends on the type of return and how complex it is. We confirm the price with you before any work begins.
        
        
        Can you help with a letter or review from the CRA?
        Yes. We help you understand the letter, gather what's requested and respond, including during GST/HST reviews and audits.
        
        
        Do you work with clients outside Canada?
        Yes, for corporate tax. We work with owners worldwide who hold Canadian real estate, shares or other business interests in Canada.
        
        
        How accurate is the tax calculator?
        It gives a close estimate using official federal and provincial rates. Your exact result depends on your own deductions and credits, which we work out when we prepare your return.
