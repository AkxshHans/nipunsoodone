    Benefits & credits

    Money many Canadians leave unclaimed.

    Most benefits are only paid if you file a return, even with little or no income. We check which of these apply to you every year.

    Canada Child Benefit

    A tax‑free monthly payment to help families with the cost of raising children under 18.

    GST/HST credit

    A tax‑free quarterly payment for individuals and families with low or modest incomes.

    Canada Workers Benefit

    A refundable credit that tops up the earnings of lower‑income workers.

    FHSA

    The First Home Savings Account: contributions are deductible, and qualifying withdrawals for a first home are tax‑free.

    Tuition credits

    Eligible tuition on your T2202 reduces your tax, and unused amounts carry forward to future years.

    Medical expenses

    Many out‑of‑pocket costs for you and your family, from prescriptions to dental work, can be claimed.

    Disability tax credit

    A credit for people with a severe and prolonged impairment, or for the family members who support them.

    Home buyers’ amount

    A credit for first‑time buyers in the year they purchase a qualifying home.

    Key dates

    Canadian tax deadlines

    The dates most of our clients plan around each year.

    • End of FebruaryT4 and T4A slips issued to employees and filed with the CRA
    • 60 days after year‑endRRSP contribution deadline for the previous tax year
    • April 30Personal returns due, and any balance owing for all individuals
    • June 15Returns due for self‑employed individuals (payment still due April 30)
    • 6 months after year‑endT2 corporate return due

    If a deadline falls on a weekend or holiday, the CRA treats it as on time the next business day. Your exact dates may differ; we confirm them for you.

    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Can’t find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll reply directly.

    Do I need to visit an office?

    No. Everything is done online, from sharing documents to approving your return. You can reach us by WhatsApp, phone or email throughout.

    I’m new to Canada. Can you help with my first return?

    Yes. Newcomers are one of the groups we work with most. We’ll explain what to report for the year you arrived and which benefits and credits you may be eligible for.

    How long does it take?

    Most returns are ready within 24–48 hours once we have complete documents. More complex returns, such as corporate filings, can take longer, and we’ll give you a timeline upfront.

    How much does it cost?

    Pricing depends on the type of return and how complex it is. We confirm the price with you before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

    Can you help if I’ve received a letter from the CRA?

    Yes. We help you understand CRA correspondence, gather what’s requested and respond, including support during GST/HST reviews and audits.

    How accurate is the tax calculator?

    It gives a close estimate of income tax using 2026 federal and provincial brackets and basic credits. Your actual result depends on CPP/EI, deductions and credits specific to you, which we calculate when preparing your return.

    Do you work with clients outside Canada?

    Yes, for corporate tax. We accept clients worldwide who hold Canadian real estate, shares in Canadian corporations, or other Canadian business interests.
